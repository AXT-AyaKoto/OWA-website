# About Us

Hello! We are **Open Web Advocacy (OWA)**.
This document is an introduction to help you learn about us.

## Q: Who are you?

A: We are a group of **people who create websites, mobile apps**, and other digital products.

For more information about our members, click [here](https://open-web-advocacy.org/ja/contributors/).

## Q: What is your goal?

A: Our goal is to **protect the future of app development** by advocating an **"open web".**

We believe that the future of app development is in danger.
Big companies like Apple and Google — which we call "gatekeepers" — charge us high fees.
But that's not all. They are also trying to monopolize the app ecosystem.
If they succeed, it will be more difficult for new ideas to emerge.
That's why we're fighting against the gatekeepers.
